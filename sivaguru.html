The short version.

I'm Siva Guru G, an aspiring actor, filmmaker, and assistant director with a deep passion for cinema and storytelling. I enjoy bringing emotions to life through acting while also exploring filmmaking from behind the camera.

My path here wasn't a straight line — I studied Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, then made the deliberate choice to pursue filmmaking fully: stepping onto sets, learning as an Assistant Director, writing screenplays, acting in short films, and editing. That analytical background still shows up in how I break down a script and plan a shoot.

My goal is simple: keep learning, keep creating meaningful stories, and build a lasting career in the film industry — one honest scene at a time.
