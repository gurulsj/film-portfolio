The short version.

I'm Siva Guru G, an actor and filmmaker — chasing one thing above all: telling stories that make people feel something real.

After finishing my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, I moved into the IT industry — working as an RPA Developer at Droidal, and later as a Machine Learning Engineer intern at Adella Software for six months. It was solid, structured work, and I don't regret a day of it — the discipline and problem-solving from that world still shape how I break down a script or plan a shoot.

But through all of it, I was quietly building something else on the side — my first short film, made in the hours after work. Every late evening went into writing scenes, planning shots, and learning direction on my own time, just because I loved it, not because anyone asked me to. Somewhere in that process it became obvious which version of my life actually felt alive — and it wasn't the one on my resume. That's when I made the deliberate choice to stop treating filmmaking as a side project and start learning it properly, fully, from the ground up: on sets, through screenwriting, acting, and editing.

My goal is simple: keep learning, keep creating meaningful stories, and build a lasting career in the film industry — one honest scene at a time.
